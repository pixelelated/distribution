## Architecture and spec fidelity

ordinary sync retains only the bounded legacy-folder presence guard. The shared
layout protocol controls copy/verify/delete and marker publication; the settings
writer and reader retain their persisted ROCKNIX archive-name contract. The

upstream with pinned native dependencies. GENERIC_X64 renderer adaptation remains
scoped to the emulated graphics boundary, not handheld Sway configuration.

scanner; layout application errors enter a transport-error mapper; and the

explain the confirmed defects without attributing them speculatively to renaming.
