> when it comes to restoring with settings, is there any risk in restoring settings from a different console? ## What exists today (read from `backuptool` at `224b73ea54`)

- A settings restore takes **the newest archive in the cloud's Backups folder, whichever device made it**: `backuptool:159-161` picks `ls …/*-ROCKNIX_SETTINGS.tar.gz | sort | tail -1`. The archive's name carries the device label (`<stamp>-<label>-ROCKNIX_SETTINGS.tar.gz`, `:143`, D-CLOUD-009) and nothing reads it back on restore. No `HW_DEVICE` comparison exists in the script.
- The archive holds `system.cfg` whole minus credential keys (`:282`, the scrub at `:416`), `retroarch/*`, `es_settings.cfg`, `fancontrol.conf`, `ppsspp/*`, `moonlight/*`, `game/*` (`:280-287`) and the systems' own configs. So the RG35XX SP's archive on the Nova brings its hostname (`system.hostname`; D-NET-001 treats any name that is not the family's as the player's own and keeps it, so the Nova would answer to the SP's name), RetroArch video and scaling settings tuned for a 640x480 H700 panel, and the H700's fan settings.
- A pre-restore snapshot is written first (`:1459`) and `revert_restore` exists (`:662`); the FINISH RESTORE SETUP wizard then asks for what was held back (the Wi-Fi password, the accounts).
- The transfer page's SETTINGS toggle names neither the device the archive came from nor that a choice exists.

Answer given at the time: do not restore the SP's settings onto the Nova; set it up fresh and let saves sync; the Nova's own settings back up from there.

## Can this be done on the VM?

**Yes.** The label is a string in the file name: `tools/cloud-test-backend seed-content` puts two labelled archives (say `Anbernic-RG35XX-SP` and `Retroid-Pocket-Nova`) in the QA cloud's Backups folder, and guest d restores as the second device. Nothing here needs a board.

## Design, 2026-09-30 (D-CLOUD-156): scan first, then offer only what this device can restore

The maintainer, later the same evening: *"if we knew that I'm on a Retroid Pocket Nova, we shouldn't show the option if there isn't a Retroid Pocket Nova backup available. Maybe the scanning the cloud step should come first, and that way we can only offer options that we can actually support in the backup. We'd have the screen real estate during the scan to also be able to say, 'We're checking to see what device-specific content and settings are available,' or something like that."*

So the per-device choice page proposed above is not the shape. The scan page (#350) runs first; the settings row is offered only when the cloud holds a settings archive whose label is this device model's (`cloud_device_id --label`, the label in the archive's name), and otherwise reads its reason. One open point for the maintainer: `es-ui-style-guide.md` dims a row that has nothing to offer rather than hiding it (`NO SETTINGS BACKUP FROM THIS DEVICE YET`, dimmed); their words say "shouldn't show the option". The row is written dimmed unless they say absent. The criteria above that describe a choice page are superseded by these:

- [ ] After the scan page (#350), the SETTINGS restore row is offered only when the cloud's Backups folder holds an archive whose label equals this device's `cloud_device_id --label`; with the QA cloud seeded with a foreign label only, guest d's 640x480 frame shows the row dimmed with its reason, and with its own label seeded the row is offered with `<DEVICE>, <DATE>` (D-CLOUD-164) under it.
- [ ] A restore never takes another device's archive by default: the cloud restore selects this device model's newest compatible archive and passes it to `backuptool`, and its journal line names the label it chose; the foreign-label case on guest d leaves `system.hostname` unchanged.
- [ ] The scan page's line reads, while it runs, the words approved for #350 (proposed: `CHECKING WHAT SETTINGS AND CONTENT YOUR CLOUD HAS FOR THIS DEVICE...`), and the outcome vocabulary when it ends (`es-player-text.md`); frames from the walk show both.
