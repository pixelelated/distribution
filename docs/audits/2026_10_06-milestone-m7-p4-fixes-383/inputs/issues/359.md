## Today

`LICENSE.md:9-11` licenses *ROCKNIX's* branding and images under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; the fork replaces that branding (D-WORKFLOW-084) and must say what its own name and artwork are offered under. The code stays GPL-2 (`LICENSE.md` § ROCKNIX Software), which governs the software, not the name. The interim icon is `docs/rasteratops/art/icon-interim-2026-10-01.png`.

## The choices (not legal advice; the decision is the maintainer's)

- **Artwork licence**: CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (the terms the fork itself accepted from ROCKNIX; a downstream fork would owe the same), CC BY-ND 4.0 (copies allowed, no adaptations), or all rights reserved (the art may not be copied at all, which every clone of a public repository technically does).
- **The name and mark**: a written trademark policy (`TRADEMARK.md`): the name and logo identify this project's own builds; unmodified builds may carry them; a modified build is renamed; no endorsement implied. Copyright on the art does not do this job; a trademark, registered or by use, does. Registration (USPTO, class 9) is a later, optional step if the project ever faces the public.
