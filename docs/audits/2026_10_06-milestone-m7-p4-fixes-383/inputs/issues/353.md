## What exists today, and the cause (read from the device and the code, 2026-09-30)

- The screen is the saves-folder offer: `cloud_restore:1729` prints `>>> offer create-saves-folder|<saves folder>` when the configured saves folder is absent but its parent lists, and `CloudOffer.cpp:59-64` shows `YOUR CLOUD HAS NO %s FOLDER YET.\n\nCREATE IT NOW?` with CREATE IT / NOT NOW. It runs from the startup sync (`GuiCloudTransfer.cpp:297`), which is why it appeared at a boot and not after the sign-in.
- **The folder it names is upstream ROCKNIX's shipped default, not ours.** The Nova ran stock ROCKNIX 20260901 before RC1, and stock ships `SYNCPATH="/GAMES"` (`upstream/next:cloud_sync.conf:18`). The fork's `cloud_sync_helper` renames the keys and keeps the values on purpose (`:393-395`, `:467-473`: *"an upgrading device keeps whatever it had - '/GAMES' for anyone set…"*, D-WORKFLOW-101's "configured root wins on upgrade"). Read from the Nova (`rocknix_ed25519`, a read): `SAVES_REMOTE="/GAMES"`, `SETTINGS_REMOTE="/GAMES/backup"`, `CONTENT_REMOTE=""` (`/storage/.config/cloud_sync.conf:67-69`, written 2026-09-29 15:35 EDT at RC1's first boot); the Dropbox holds `/ROCKNIX/{Backups,Content,Saves,Saves-replaced}` and **no `/GAMES`**. So the device offers to create a folder the player never chose beside the one their other devices use, and its content root, empty, is the Dropbox root: **this is the cause of #352's listing too.**
- A `/GAMES` typed by hand is refused (`cloud_setup:350-366`: the saves folder needs a parent); a `/GAMES` carried across an upgrade is never checked.
- The dialog's icon is `ICON_AUTOMATIC` (`GuiMsgBox.h:15`), chosen by the trailing `?`; the "question mark separating the two rows" is that icon's placement at the Nova's panel size, to be seen on a frame.

The remedy on the Nova tonight is one line on the maintainer's yes: `cloud_setup --set-saves-remote /ROCKNIX/Saves` writes the three fork paths (`cloud_setup:629-631`) and moves nothing in the cloud.

## Can this be done on the VM?

**Yes.** Guest d boots the previous stock-shaped conf (`SYNCPATH="/GAMES"`) through `tools/vm-upgrade-rehearsal`'s seeding, with the QA cloud holding `/ROCKNIX/Saves` and no `/GAMES`; the offer's frame at 640x480 and the journal are the observation. The naming question is the maintainer's.
