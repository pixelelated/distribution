The old rc-accept exception is not renewed. Current scope: docs/rasteratops/release-readiness.md.

Found by the VM on 2026-09-28 while proving #307's PL-069 on guest d (build `4234be0b6b`, GENERIC_X64 at 640x480 on GL; the runner's report `docs/qa-frames/2026-09-28/proofs-307/run1.md`, row E1 PL-069). PL-069's own claim holds: the exit sync's detached thread no longer leaks (the control run with `cloudsaves.gameexit=0` grows exactly as the run with the sync on). What does not hold is the interface's launch/exit cycle itself:

- 50 launches of `gb/Ninoid.gb` and exits, the interface's pid 1564 throughout, threads 21 throughout: **VmSize 2228576 -> 2739888 kB, +10240 kB on every launch/exit cycle** (`logs/E1-pl069.csv`), VmRSS 206448 -> 229540 kB (about +0.5 MB a cycle).
- The control, the same loop with no sync and no `ThreadedCloudSync` thread: VmSize 2228484 -> 2321984 kB over 10 cycles, the same +10240 kB a cycle (`logs/E1-pl069-control.csv`).

Ten mebibytes of address space a game, resident growth of about half a megabyte a game, on a handheld with one gigabyte: a long evening of many games leaves the interface hundreds of megabytes larger in its map and tens of megabytes larger in memory, until the kernel's killer or a restart. The shape (a fixed 10 MiB per cycle, threads flat) reads like a stack-sized mapping per launch that is never unmapped -- a detached thread's stack from the launch path, or a mapping the launch makes and forgets -- but the cause is not found.

Can this be done on the VM? Yes -- the runner's `E1-pl069-control.sh` reproduces it in ten cycles; `/proc/<pid>/maps` diffed across one cycle names the mapping.

## Remaining live-heap source identified — 2026-10-03 06:06 UTC

Allocator snapshots across ten more launches show about1.65MiB additional
live heap. LeakSanitizer on the isolated VM then reports1,153,399 leaked bytes
in14,360 allocations after ten launches. The dominant stacks resolve with
the exact unstripped diagnostic binary to `getDeviceParentSyspath` in
InputConfig.cpp:73, called for keyboard, gun and mouse configurations at each
Window::init. Each scan loses its `udev_enumerate` reference, retaining the
enumerated device list. The same missing unref exists in isWheel, getMice and
initial gun enumeration. These are inherited source defects, still present
in the current ES feature source. Fix all owned enumeration lifetimes,
preserve copied fallback paths, and repeat the sanitizer and noninstrumented
10/50-cycle controls. Raw report: `/tmp/rasteratops-m7-memory/lsan-report-10.log`.


The identical LeakSanitizer control after the four ES enumeration fixes drops
from1,153,399 bytes/14,360 objects to15,768 bytes/28 objects; no udev frames
remain. It also exposes two smaller repeat-per-launch library leaks: SDL2's
Wayland display removal loses its mode array (768 bytes/cycle), and Mesa's
virgl screen cache retains an empty table after a failed software-profile
probe (592 bytes/cycle). Both are still present in upstream source inspected
today. Contained cleanup patches and the same before/after control will
cover these before final noninstrumented endurance acceptance. One-time
shutdown allocations are distinguished from cycle-dependent leaks.
