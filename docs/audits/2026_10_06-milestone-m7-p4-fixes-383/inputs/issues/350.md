# M7.P3: The content comparison loader is drawn over the Restore from cloud dialog; make it an interstitial page

Source: https://github.com/pixelelated/distribution/issues/350
Updated: 2026-10-02T23:05:24Z

## Readiness review update — 2026-10-02 (#375)

D-CLOUD-167 supersedes the earlier after-CONTINUE placement: opening either transfer page runs the initial scan before options; CONTINUE with content selected performs the subsequent content scan for the selected classes. Completed scan pages advance automatically; failures retain their outcome and TRY AGAIN/CLOSE. D-CLOUD-164 approved the scan strings. Capture this full sequence in the final candidate's proof; `docs/retros/2026-10-02-cloud-runs-95-101.md` identifies isolated case reruns as insufficient.

## The maintainer's words (2026-09-30, on the Retroid Pocket Nova)

> The "Comparing your cloud's content with this device" loader is a loader that appears over the "Restore from cloud" screen, which violates our design practice of having a pop-up over a pop-up. This should probably be its own screen that we show as an interstitial when the user hits Continue and before we show any options.

## What exists today

The CONTENT TO RESTORE flow confirms with a dialog and then draws the comparison's busy card over it while `cloud_content_restore` lists the cloud (the loader text is `COMPARING YOUR CLOUD'S CONTENT WITH THIS DEVICE`). Two surfaces stacked for one event is the anti-pattern `es-native-ui.md` § Anti-patterns names, and a job the player waits on gets a page of its own (the fourth tier, D-UI-078).

## Can this be done on the VM?

**Yes.** Guest d against `tools/cloud-test-backend` with seeded content; the walk `tools/vm-walks` presses Continue and captures the frame sequence at 640x480.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Opening BACK UP TO THE CLOUD or RESTORE FROM THE CLOUD opens CHECKING YOUR CLOUD before any options (D-CLOUD-167), with the live line and CANCEL as the one way out while it runs (D-UI-078); the options page follows when the listing is in. A `tools/vm-walks` frame sequence shows menu, scan page, options, and no frame with a card drawn over a dialog.
- [ ] When the comparison fails (the cloud unreachable: the dead port of `tools/cloud-test-backend`), the page says why in the outcome vocabulary (`COULDN'T FINISH - …`, `es-player-text.md`) and offers TRY AGAIN beside CLOSE; a frame shows it.
- [ ] `docs/es-menu-map.md` carries the page (D-UI-039); `tools/es-menu-map-check` passes.
- [ ] The interface edit passes `tools/es-syntax-check` before the pin moves, and `docs/cloud-sync-changelog.md` carries the change the day it lands.

## Design, 2026-09-30 (D-CLOUD-156): the scan page is where the offer is decided

The maintainer, later the same evening: *"Maybe the scanning the cloud step should come first, and that way we can only offer options that we can actually support in the backup. We'd have the screen real estate during the scan to also be able to say, 'We're checking to see what device-specific content and settings are available,' or something like that."*

So this page is not only a loader moved out from under a dialog: it runs before any option is shown, checks the settings archives by device label (#349) and the content folder under the cloud root (#352), and the options page that follows lists only what the scan found. Added criteria:

- [ ] The opening scan checks folder state, settings archives by label and content location before options; CONTINUE with content selected runs the second content scan in the selected classes (D-CLOUD-167); the options page on guest d lists only rows the scan found (a frame per seeded case: settings for this label, settings for a foreign label only, content under `/ROCKNIX/Content`, content nowhere).
- [ ] Its live line says what it is checking in the words approved for it (D-CLOUD-164: `CHECKING WHAT SETTINGS AND CONTENT YOUR CLOUD HAS FOR THIS DEVICE...`); the string and its French land in the same commit (D-UI-051).
