## What holds the pointers
- SystemScreenSaver keeps mGamesWithImages, mGamesWithVideos (std::vector<FileData*>) and mCurrentGame (SystemScreenSaver.h:117-118, :126), filled by countGameListNodes (SystemScreenSaver.cpp:321-370) from every game system's getFilesRecursive, read by pickRandomGameMedia (:399-430) and selectGameMedia (:372-396), and mCurrentGame by getCurrentGame (:531) and launchGame (:536-550, with ScreenSaverControls a launch).
- Only the slideshow and random-video behaviours fill the lists; dim and black never call countGameListNodes.

## What clears them
- resetCounts (SystemScreenSaver.h:95) clears both lists and their loaded flags. Its one caller is Window::cancelScreenSaver (Window.cpp:1107-1117), when the screensaver stops on input.
- mCurrentGame is set to NULL at each start (:149, :203, :227) and at each pick (:401), not at a rescan.

## What deletes the games
- SystemData::rescanIfFolderChanged (SystemData.cpp:378-512): its `vanished` (:459-473) drops collection entries and runs `delete n` for a game or folder gone from disk. It drops and remakes the system's game list view (#246's rule) but tells the screensaver nothing.
- Its callers: SystemData::rescanChangedFolders (:514-518), posted to the interface thread after a game exits (FileData.cpp:1223) and when a transfer ends (guis/GuiCloudTransfer.cpp:335).

## The window
- After a game, the screensaver is not running when the posted rescan runs: the launch stopped it, and the exit is input.
- During a transfer it can be: Window starts the screensaver after ScreenSaverTime without input (Window.cpp:777-779), and nothing in GuiCloudTransfer holds it off. A transfer measured in minutes (D-UI-078) outlasts the default. A restore or a match that removes ROMs (cloud_content_restore --match) makes `vanished` delete games the slideshow's list still holds. The next picture, or a press with ScreenSaverControls on mCurrentGame, reads freed memory.
- The list is therefore not cleared on that path, which is the brief's condition for a fixture.
