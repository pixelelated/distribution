Title: Bug: an interrupted Steam first launch leaves x86 and box64 binfmt disabled

Found by the M6 T3 lane (#650), F-22, punch item PL-021. Low. `deferred:M7-merge`: the M7 lane's `6101242e14` changes this file.

## Code trace

- **How it was found:** the slice's read of `Install Steam.sh`, a lead re-read at its lines.
- **The defect at its source:** `run_steam_first_launch` (`projects/ROCKNIX/packages/virtual/emulators/sources/Install Steam.sh` 2692 to 2710) writes `0` to `/proc/sys/fs/binfmt_misc/{x86,box32,box64}` (2694 to 2696). It restores them only with `systemctl restart systemd-binfmt` at its end (2709), and the file has no `trap` (`grep -c -E '^\s*trap '` 0).
- **What happens:** an interrupt or kill while Steam runs (2703; the player closing the terminal) leaves x86 and box64 binfmt off until a reboot or a restart of that unit.
- **The fix:** `trap 'systemctl restart systemd-binfmt' EXIT INT TERM` around the block, removed after the restart.
- **Sibling sites hunted:** the file's other `/proc` and service writes: `swaymsg` seats (2699, 2706), with the same shape, and the `set_kill` file.
- **What reading cannot prove:** which launches the disabled binfmt breaks in between.

Already written: nothing persistent; `/proc` state is reset at boot.

Can this be done on the VM? **Yes, in part**: a guest run of the function with a stub client that is killed, then the binfmt entries read.
