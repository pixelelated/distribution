Title: Bug: `Install Steam.sh` installs the Steam runtime and Proton archives with no integrity check

Found by the M6 T3 lane (#650), F-21, punch item PL-009. Medium. `deferred:M7-merge`: the M7 lane's `6101242e14` changes this file, and the fix is planned over the merged file.

## Code trace

- **How it was found:** the slice's read of `Install Steam.sh`, through a research subagent's leads, each re-read at its lines.
- **The defect at its source:**
  - **The runtime:** its version string comes from `curl -fsSL` (1060 to 1062) and its archive from `wget -t 3 -T 30 -O <work>/runtime.tar.xz` (1069 to 1071). Only the archive's structure and ELF headers are checked afterwards.
  - **Proton:** `wget` at 2630, then only `0 < size <= MAX_ARCHIVE` (2632). The release's `.sha512sum` asset is deliberately skipped (222) and never fetched.
  - **The client, by contrast,** is checked against its manifest's SHA-256 and size (1749 to 1751).
- **The fix:**
  - fetch and check Proton's `.sha512sum`;
  - check the runtime against the checksum file Valve publishes beside its images, if one exists;
  - restrict redirects to HTTPS on those channels, as the client transfer does (`--proto =https --proto-redir =https`).
- **Sibling sites hunted:** every download in the file: `FEXRootFSFetcher`'s output (its own tool's job), the runtime, the client, and Proton twice.
- **What reading cannot prove:** whether `wget` is busybox's on the image, and its certificate behaviour.

Already written: installed runtimes and Protons under `/storage` stay; the check applies to the next download.

Can this be done on the VM? **Yes**: a guest run of the Proton step against a local HTTPS fixture whose `.sha512sum` does not match its archive.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On the guest, with a fixture release whose `.sha512sum` does not match its archive, `install_proton_variant` refuses with a named reason and installs nothing; with a matching one, it installs.
- [ ] The runtime download is checked against a published checksum, or the script states in a comment beside the download why none exists.
