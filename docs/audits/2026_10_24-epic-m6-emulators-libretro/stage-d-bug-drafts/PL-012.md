Title: Bug: `sameboy-lr` references `PKG_BUILD` at global scope, for an option its libretro build never reads (`pkgcheck` FAIL)

Found by the M6 T3 lane (#650), F-02, punch item PL-012. Low.

## Code trace

- **How it was found:** `tools/pkgcheck` over every recipe of the slice, 152 runs. One exits 1: `[FAIL] 013: projects/ROCKNIX/packages/emulators/libretro/sameboy-lr/package.mk: late binding violation : ref PKG_BUILD`.
- **The defect at its source:** `sameboy-lr/package.mk:13`, `PKG_MAKE_OPTS_TARGET="-C libretro BOOTROMS_DIR=${PKG_BUILD}/BootROMs/prebuilt"`. `PKG_BUILD` is empty when the recipe loads (`packages/README.md` § Late Binding), so the option is `BOOTROMS_DIR=/BootROMs/prebuilt`.
- **Why it has no effect today:** SameBoy's `libretro/Makefile` (the pin `aa158a889a`, as unpacked in the M7 GENERIC_X64 build root) takes its boot ROMs from `$(CORE_DIR)/BootROMs/prebuilt/` (its lines 368 and 381). Only the top-level `Makefile` reads `BOOTROMS_DIR` (line 56), and the recipe builds `-C libretro`. The core is built and installed.
- **The fix:** drop `BOOTROMS_DIR=...` and keep `-C libretro`.
- **Sibling sites hunted:** the 151 other recipes of the slice; `pkgcheck` passes every one.
- **What reading cannot prove:** that no SameBoy version after the pin reads the option in its libretro build. A bump reads its Makefile again.

Already written: nothing inherited (a build option).

Can this be done on the VM? **n/a**: a recipe. The proof is `pkgcheck` now and the core's presence in the next image.
