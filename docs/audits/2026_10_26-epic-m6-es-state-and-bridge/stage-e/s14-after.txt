## The self-signed listener: CANNOT on this host
The brief's second positive -- HttpReq against a self-signed listener, accepted on the pin and refused after -- needs HttpReq built and linked against libcurl. This host has libcurl's runtime library (libcurl-gnutls.so.4) and no headers; the stand-in header in stage-e/fakes/ declares names with invented option codes, which is fine for -fsyntax-only and would make a linked binary send curl the wrong options, a test of nothing true. So the listener is CANNOT here, named for the integration window's guest walk (T20's guest-cycle/scripts/tls-probe.py is the listener's shape), where the image's HttpReq and libcurl run.
