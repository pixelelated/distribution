## Code trace

**Where (at `16dbac5211`):** `Log.cpp:84-99` (`emergencyFlush`, its flush `:98`), `:237-242` (`crashHandler`), `:282-285` (the registrations) against the worker `:117-162`

**The defect:** `crashHandler`, registered for SIGABRT and SIGTERM, calls `emergencyFlush`, which sets `mEmergency`, notifies the worker, waits up to 200 x 50 us for it, and then calls `mFile.flush()` itself, on the handler's thread. The worker may be writing the same `std::ofstream` at that moment (`drainBuffer`/`writeLine`, and its own flush at `:147`), and nothing orders the two: a data race on the stream's buffer, which can garble the log's last lines or fault inside the handler. `notify_one` and `sleep_for` are not async-signal-safe either.

**How it was found:** T35's audit (#682): the independent seat's G-4, verified at the pin; PL-28 in `docs/audits/2026_10_31-epic-m6-es-core-window-input/05-punch-list.md` and #1337. Ruled at the stage-D pause (ruling 10): the handler's own flush goes only if a ThreadSanitizer program shows the race on `mFile` at the pin; it did, so this bug is filed before the stream's commit (ruling 15).

**The positive before the fix:** `SAN tsan-log-flush (-fsanitize=thread): rc 66; WARNING: ThreadSanitizer: data race` (`stage-e/s7-before.txt`, `stage-e/tsan-log-flush.cpp` over `Log.cpp:29-196` lifted verbatim): the handler thread's `write` of the file buffer (`std::__basic_file<char>::xsputn`) against the worker's `memcpy` into it (`std::basic_streambuf<char>::xsputn` under `AsyncLogger::workerLoop`), the buffer allocated by `std::basic_filebuf<char>::_M_allocate_internal_buffer`.

**What reading cannot prove:** how often a crash lands while the worker writes on a device.

**Can this be done on the VM?** Yes -- host-only (the ThreadSanitizer program in `tools/t35-build-tests.sh`).
