## Code trace

**Where (at `16dbac5211`):** `Window.cpp:1262-1274` against `:1276-1290`, `:1292-1307`

**The defect:** `postToUiThread` appends under `mNotificationMessagesLock` and `processPostedFunctions` copies and clears under it; `unregisterPostedFunctions` iterates and erases `mFunctions` with no lock. A worker posting while a page unregisters reallocates the vector under the erase loop: container corruption, undefined behaviour.

**How it was found:** T35's audit (#682): L-03 (the first reader), confirmed by the seat at Medium; PL-07 in `docs/audits/2026_10_31-epic-m6-es-core-window-input/05-punch-list.md` and #1337.

**The positive before the fix:** `SAN tsan-posts (-fsanitize=thread): rc 66; WARNING: ThreadSanitizer: data race` -- the erase loop's read on one thread against the post's reallocation under the mutex on the other (`stage-c/tsan-posts.cpp` over `Window.cpp:1262-1274` and `:1276-1290` lifted verbatim; `stage-c/build-tests.out`).

**What reading cannot prove:** which pages unregister while a worker posts (the callers are es-app's).

**Can this be done on the VM?** Yes -- host-only (the ThreadSanitizer program in `tools/t35-build-tests.sh`).
