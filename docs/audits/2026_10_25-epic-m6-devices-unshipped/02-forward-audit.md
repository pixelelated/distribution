## Findings

### F-01 (Q6, Q7; Low; Renovation; the inventory's reach, a lead): the kernel's unpack edges are invisible to the plan-derived reach

- **The shape.** `projects/ROCKNIX/packages/linux/package.mk` takes two of the slice's recipes through edges a build plan does not list.
  - `:69-72`: on any x86 `TARGET_ARCH`, `PKG_DEPENDS_UNPACK+=" intel-ucode kernel-firmware"`, and `:202` copies `$(get_build_dir intel-ucode)/intel-ucode` into `external-firmware`, which `:204-207` lists into `CONFIG_EXTRA_FIRMWARE`.
  - `:81-82` unpacks `generic-dsi` for RK3326 and RK3566, and `:108-111` copies its source into the kernel and compiles it for RK3326, RK3566 and RK3576.
- **Which copy each target resolves.** `get_pkg_directory` searches `projects/ROCKNIX/devices/${DEVICE}/packages` and `projects/ROCKNIX/packages` before `packages/` (`config/functions:789-798`, `:1057-1079`). So AMD64 takes its device copy of `intel-ucode`, and GENERIC_X64, which has none, takes `packages/linux-firmware/intel-ucode`.
- **The record agrees.** T11's audit counted **153 `intel-ucode` entries** in the GENERIC_X64 guest kernel's `CONFIG_EXTRA_FIRMWARE` (`feature/m6-device-generic-x64-658` at `ed8c48f3cd`, `docs/audits/2026_10_25-epic-m6-device-generic-x64-and-kernel/02-forward-audit.md:142`).
- **What the inventory says.** All four `intel-ucode` rows are `unreached`, `buildable_by []`, and `generic-dsi`'s three rows list RK3326 alone.
- **Effect on this slice.**
  - The generic pair is *reached after all*, by the test image.
  - AMD64's pair is `unshipped: target AMD64`.
  - `generic-dsi`'s rows gain RK3566 and RK3576. Their reach is unchanged.
  - **The dead class shrinks from 207 to 203 rows** (172 + 30 + 1, against the brief's 172 + 32 + 3).
- **What it is not.** It is not a defect in an image: both edges do what they are written to do. It is a reach the inventory's plan runs cannot see. **The ladder:** `tools/renovation-inventory` reads `buildable_by` from plan steps, and an unpack-only edge is not a step. This is a shape it misses, in the opposite direction to #1086's (which reads shipped where nothing reads). That makes it a lead for the coordinator, not a tool change here (brief § 4).
- **Already written:** nothing; the inventory is regenerated, not migrated.
