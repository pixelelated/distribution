## Code trace

- **How it was found:** F-09 in `02-forward-audit.md`, read at `8ebaf8daad`; evidence: the trace in `02` F-09; three M7 `target/` directories.
- **The defect at its source:** `build_distro` prunes the device's outputs before `scripts/image`, so an identity's bytes can be replaced on the documented route. Where: `scripts/build_distro:82-84`; `scripts/image:78-82`.
- **The fix's shape:** Refuse in `build_distro`, before the prune, when `BUILD_TIMESTAMP` is set and `target/` already holds an output of that identity; or prune only outputs of another timestamp. The stream brief chooses. Write `.identity.json` after the image's checksum, not before `do_mkimage` (Argon G-02).
- **Sibling sites:** named in the finding; any further site is listed here when the fix's stream brief hunts them.
- **What reading cannot prove:** what only a build shows; this issue's image proof is the coordinator's first build after the merge (`fix-pending-image`) unless the criterion below is a sandbox's.
- **Already written:** candidates live in the immutable store, which `build_distro` never touches; a `target/` replaced in the past is not detectable now; nothing to migrate

**Can this be done on the VM?** No: a device build on the host; a sandbox of `build_distro`'s prune and the image's refusal over a fixture `target/` (fix-sandbox), then a rebuild with a frozen timestamp (fix-pending-image)
