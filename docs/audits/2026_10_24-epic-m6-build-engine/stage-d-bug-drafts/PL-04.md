## Code trace

- **How it was found:** F-03 in `02-forward-audit.md`, read at `8ebaf8daad`; evidence: P6, P7.
- **The defect at its source:** `add_es_system` carries one system's fragment into the next: `daphne` and `gp32` ship the wrong year, and a system appended after `imageviewer` would launch nothing. Where: `distributions/ROCKNIX/config/functions:127-142`; `config/emulators/{daphne,gp32,arcade,segamodel3}.conf`.
- **The fix's shape:** Unset the ten `SYSTEM_*` keys and `SYSTEM_COMMAND` at the top of `add_es_system`; give `config/emulators/daphne.conf` and `gp32.conf` their `SYSTEM_RELEASE` (1983, 2001) and `arcade.conf`, `segamodel3.conf` their `SYSTEM_WIKI_PATH`.
- **Sibling sites:** named in the finding; any further site is listed here when the fix's stream brief hunts them.
- **What reading cannot prove:** what only a build shows; this issue's image proof is the coordinator's first build after the merge (`fix-pending-image`) unless the criterion below is a sandbox's.
- **Already written:** every image built carries `release="1996"` for both systems; the table is regenerated at each image and nothing on a device stores it, so the next image corrects it; nothing to migrate

**Can this be done on the VM?** Yes: the host regeneration (`tools/regen-es-systems.sh`) shows the table; the guest shows the year in the system view after the build (fix-pending-image for the two shipped lines)
