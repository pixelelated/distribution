## What we discovered

- **The Nova's whole boot path is superseded upstream** (`0340192729` to `73ffb8ee37`). Upstream's entry would be `pixelelated.conf` on the fork, and upstream's updater keeps the failure-masking shape.
- **SM8550 is the one Qualcomm sibling whose GPU firmware is not embedded,** with its runtime observed only narrowly.
- **The Nova's tree is chosen by a rule written nowhere,** among seven with its identifiers.
- **The Nova's touchscreen selects pinctrl for gpio24/25 on gpio14/15 pins** (upstream's tree; a #585 line).
