## What was harder than expected

- **Ruling 14's wording against what was observed.** The ruling expected
  the busybox pair's FAIL "before the fix". It showed at the fix instead:
  `moved=0` at the parent, then `moved=1 touched=0`. Both readings are
  recorded in `02` § The streams. The ruling describes the same outcome
  from the other side; nothing changed in the evidence.
- **The first `rc` lines in `five-checkers.txt` were taken after a pipe**,
  which the session's rules forbid. The file was rewritten with each `rc`
  on its own line. Three checkers are cited from their 03:11:46Z
  standalone runs. The rule was known and still slipped into a collection
  script that was written quickly.
- **One `rm -rf` broke the removal form.** At 03:00 a mistyped
  `rm -rf "/workspace/tmp/m6-t37/e:?"` ran inside a longer command. It
  named a path that does not exist and removed nothing (`00`, stage E).
  The form `"${E:?}"/...`, in its own short command, was used for every
  real retirement before and after it.
- **Two of the five new assertions had no mutation until the retro.**
  Stage E added one mutation per assertion *group*, so
  `using-lines-print-no-value` (a check that the build log carries no
  credential value) had only ever PASSed. Its PASS meant nothing until a
  mutation printing the value was seen to fail it. That happened at
  03:21, along with `armsx2-filter-matches-armsx2-turnip`'s mutation.
- **The merge's `--no-commit` step ran with `-c core.hooksPath=/dev/null`.**
  The commit itself ran with the hooks, and `00` says so. A merge recipe
  that never turns the hooks off would remove the question.

## Practice adjustments (proposals for the coordinator)

- **A kit helper that writes `rc=` on its own line** for a list of commands.
  It would remove the rewrite of `five-checkers.txt`. This lane wrote no
  tool for it (learning-capture § 3: a procedure becomes a tool).
- **A retirement helper**, `kit/retire.sh <dir>`, that records the size and
  time, removes the directory with the `"${VAR:?}"` form, and refuses a
  path outside `/workspace/tmp/<lane>/`. The 03:00 slip would then have
  been refused rather than harmless.
- **One mutation per assertion, not per group**, as a line in the
  FAIL-first rule (D-WORKFLOW-211). A control that only ever passes is a
  check with no observed positive.
- **A stream brief that names where a stamp-input fix's expected FAIL
  appears**: at the parent, or at the fix with a different count. This
  would avoid a mismatch like ruling 14's.
